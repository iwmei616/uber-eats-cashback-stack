## What an Uber Eats Promo Code Actually Is (and Isn't)

A promo code on Uber Eats is a short alphanumeric string — something like `US36J5H` or `eats-jessicag29184ue` — that you paste into the "Promo Code" field in your account (tap your name, select "Promos," paste, hit "Apply") or enter at checkout. The code applies a discount to a qualifying order: a flat dollar amount off, a percentage off, free delivery, or a bundle like "$15 off your first order of $20 or more."

What it isn't: a permanent price cut. Most codes are single-use, tied to a specific user account, and often targeted — meaning a code that worked for someone on Reddit may show "expired" or "not eligible" when you try it. The Uber Eats subreddit runs a monthly "Existing User Promo Code Thread" precisely because codes rotate, get pulled, and are sometimes region-locked. Treating any single code as a guaranteed win is the fastest way to be disappointed.

The codes worth chasing fall into three buckets:

- **New-user codes**: The biggest discounts live here. Uber Eats wants first orders, so they offer things like $15 off a $20+ first order, $20 off your first two orders, or 50% off (up to $20) a first order over $15. These are one-time and tied to your account being genuinely new.
- **Existing-user codes**: Smaller and more fragile. Often $5–$10 off a $25+ order, or "5 free deliveries" promos that show up in the app's Promotions section or via email. These are the ones that expire fastest and are most likely to be targeted.
- **Free-delivery promos**: Either a $0 Delivery Fee code or a section of the app where merchants offer free delivery on orders over a threshold (often $10). Uber One members get $0 delivery on eligible orders within 3 miles, but that's a membership benefit, not a code.
